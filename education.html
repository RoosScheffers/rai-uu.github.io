Theses

Doing a thesis with us

Students work on real research questions: some theses become papers at major conferences, and many use data or cases from our partners, such as the Dutch National Police and the Netherlands Red Cross. Below is a selection of recent theses supervised in the group, with links to the thesis and its code.

  1. Find a topic. Browse our research themes and the example theses below, or bring your own idea.
  2. Contact a supervisor. Email the staff member whose work fits your interest best; see the team page.
  3. Shape the project. Together we define a research question, often linked to an ongoing project or partner.
  4. Share the results. We encourage publishing code and data openly, and we collect the repositories on github.com/rai-uu.
